Focus Kit is a calm workspace that clears the noise between you and the thing you're actually trying to build. No dashboards to manage. No metrics to feed. Just a quiet session, a timer, and the work.

One session.
Three quiet parts.

  1. 01

    Set a length

    Choose 15, 25, or 45 minutes — whatever matches the shape of the task in front of you.

  2. 02

    Name the work

    One line describing what "done" looks like. It keeps the session honest.

  3. 03

    Begin

    The timer runs. Everything else can wait until it's finished.
